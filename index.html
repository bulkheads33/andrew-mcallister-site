On-site Execution

One week on site each month for six months

I drove complex vessel work including ballast-water treatment installation. Across the business I negotiated four shipyard contracts totaling over $40M, including two over $12M each, and more than 50 vendor relationships.

Capital Structure

Refinancing with the CFO

$8M+ annual cash availability

I worked with McAllister’s CFO on bank negotiations, ownership confidence, syndication and hedges matched to revolver exposure. Removing scheduled principal amortization and reducing interest created room to invest in the business.

Technology and People

Build the operation, then convert

NetSuite / HELM / vessel connectivity

NetSuite had been selected before I arrived. I deferred conversion until operating procedures stabilized, while deploying the full HELM suite as vessels moved under in-house management. I also filled gaps in vessel computers, software and communications to support safe operations.

Commercial and Safety

I led the groups that built the Safety Management System and passed audits, served as executive representative for the ISO 9001 audit, and supported U.S. Coast Guard and American Bureau of Shipping (ABS) approvals to meet demanding maritime safety and vessel standards.

Team-led Transformation

Ticketing / reporting / restaurant operations

04 The CFO Role Is Changing

Start with the operating problem.

My approach to AI follows the same discipline as any business change: understand the goal, map the process, involve the people doing the work and establish accountability. My current AI learning includes Anthropic coursework and practical exploration.

Understand the work

Find the friction before choosing a tool.

Talk with the teams, trace the information and understand where decisions stall. Fix unclear responsibilities and broken processes before automating them.

Apply technology with judgment

Test, learn and give people ownership.

Use a bounded pilot, reliable information and human review. Measure whether the change improves the work, then build the team’s ability to sustain it.

Data collection Connect source information and agree on definitions before relying on automated analysis.
Reporting cadence Improve timely visibility while preserving formal owner and board responsibilities.
Workflow visibility Make approvals, exceptions and handoffs clear to the people accountable for them.
Executive work Keep human judgment, security and responsibility central to each decision.
